How it's calculated

Live: chance = base × (1 + 0.15 × Looting). If that's below 5%, it's then × (1 + MF/100), with MF capped at 900. Looting doesn't apply to pets; pets add Pet Luck to MF. Over 100% means extra copies.

Alpha: every mob drop gets MF (cap 500). Looting I–VII is now flat +1/2/3/4/6/8/10 MF. For drops under 100%: chance = base × (1 + MF/100); anything over 100% is a guaranteed drop plus a chance of another (e.g. 122 MF on a 50% drop gives 1 + 11%). For 100% drops, you get MF/100 extra items (8 Pumpkins at 145 MF = 9.45 on average).

Mobs not in the patch notes keep their live base rates (from the wiki), but MF now applies to all of their drops, so their real rates still change. Rift mobs (no MF there) and Garden pests are left out.

Mob-type MF (shards, Griffin, Megalodon, Sorrow Armor…) is added to your Alpha MF only on mobs with that type, before the 500 cap. It isn't added to live, since the live MF you enter already includes whatever you use. Some mobs have no type on the wiki and get none.

"Change" compares the average items per kill. Amount ranges from the wiki (e.g. 2–4) are averaged.

Assumptions: Pet Luck is assumed to still add to MF for pets on Alpha. For drops under 100% with more than one item, extra rolls are assumed to give the whole stack. Zombie Slayer uses Hypixel's published Alpha sheet; its live rates are worked out from the wiki's weights at max Slayer level. Slayer boss loot (from the wiki's weight tables) ignores Looting and only gets MF on drops under 5%. Tarantula, Sven, Voidgloom and Inferno keep that system on Alpha (Hypixel is trialling the new one on Revenant first), so only your MF and their dye rates change. Riftstalker is in the Rift, where MF doesn't apply, so only its dye change is listed. Other dyes, Dungeon chests, and tables marked "No Changes" aren't included. Mobs that share a name with a different wiki page (Hub Skeleton, Private Island Enderman, Crystal Hollows Goblin, Hub Rat) have no live data.
