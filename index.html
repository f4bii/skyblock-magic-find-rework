How it's calculated

Live: chance = base × (1 + 0.15 × Looting). If that's below 5%, it's then × (1 + MF/100), with MF capped at 900. Looting doesn't apply to pets; pets add Pet Luck to MF. Over 100% means extra copies.

Alpha (0.27.3 RC): every mob drop gets MF (cap 500). Looting I–VII is now flat +2.5/5/7.5/10/15/20/25 MF. Chance = base × (1 + MF/100); anything past 100% is a chance at another full stack. Hypixel's example: 150 MF on 8× Lily Pad (100%) gives 16 guaranteed and a 50% chance of 24 (20 on average).

Mobs not in the patch notes keep their live base rates (from the wiki), but MF now applies to all of their drops, so their real rates still change. Rift mobs (no MF there, except Vampire Slayer levels on the Riftstalker) and Garden pests are left out.

Mob-type MF (shards, Griffin, Megalodon, Sorrow Armor…) is added to your Alpha MF only on mobs with that type, before the 500 cap. It isn't added to live, since the live MF you enter already includes whatever you use. Some mobs have no type on the wiki and get none.

"Change" compares the average items per kill. Amount ranges from the wiki (e.g. 2–4) are averaged.

Slayers: on Alpha every slayer boss (Vampire included) uses a normal table where each drop rolls on its own, from Hypixel's published sheets plus the Nov 12 changes. Live rates are worked out from the wiki's weight tables at max Slayer level; those ignore Looting and only get MF on drops under 5%. Slayer levels now give MF on their own boss (set per slayer under Mob-type MF). In the Rift only Vampire Slayer level MF counts, and live had none.

Assumptions: Pet Luck is assumed to still add to MF for pets on Alpha. Ender Dragon drops assume 4 Summoning Eyes placed. Other dyes, Dungeon chests, and tables marked "No Changes" aren't included. Mobs that share a name with a different wiki page (Hub Skeleton, Private Island Enderman, Crystal Hollows Goblin, Hub Rat) have no live data. The Clover Helmet's "double or nothing" isn't modelled: Hypixel is reverting it to +5 MF before release.
